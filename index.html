AC-130 SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

You are dropped from altitude into a random 3 km sector. Extraction is at least 2.5 km away — this is a walk you have to survive.

An AC-130 gunship sweeps the whole sector on patrol. It does not know where you are — but if its circle passes over you in the open, it sees you, and it will not leave once it has your scent.

Break line of sight: buildings, tree canopy, ridges, cliffs and craters. Crouch and stay still and the detection meter drains again.

When the meter fills it is LOCKED and opens fire. 105 mm levels everything within 5-10 m — buildings collapse to rubble, trees are felled, the ground itself craters. 50 mm is a fast burst that kills on a hit. The blast will throw you if it doesn't kill you.

There is no timer. Reach the extraction zone without being killed.
